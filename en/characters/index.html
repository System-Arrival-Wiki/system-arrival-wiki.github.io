Dog

Companion

A friendly dog found tied to a fence in a parking lot. Once freed, it joins Nathan on expeditions and waits in its kennel at the bunker between them. Has its own 10-slot backpack and four modes: Heel (stays by your side), Search (finds fractals and loots safe containers and corpses), Guard (attacks enemies that target you or the dog, within 8 m of you) and Sic 'em (hunts enemies within 40 m of you). It can also be told to attack a chosen enemy, search a chosen container, wait or come back.

No stats or starting psi ability in the game data.
