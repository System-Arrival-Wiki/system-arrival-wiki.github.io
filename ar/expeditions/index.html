<en> Expeditions

<en> How it works

  1. <en> Pick a resident and a location. <en> Open the map in the bunker, choose a resident and a location, and check the danger preview before departure.
  2. <en> Choose a goal and a behaviour. <en> The goal decides what can be found. The behaviour sets the risk, ammo use and XP.
  3. <en> Take control. <en> The player can take over control of the resident. Starting at the nearest node on the selected map with the inventory and loadout of the resident.
  4. <en> Wait for the return. <en> The resident returns on their own. The notice says whether loot was brought back, lost or not found.
  5. <en> Read the report. <en> It lists the loot, fractals by tier, injuries, ammo spent and gear wear.

المتطلبات

  • تُفتح الحملات المستقلة بعد الدرس التعليمي.
  • الصحة منخفضة جدا (< 30%)
  • لا يمكن إرسال الشخصية: ليست في الملجأ.
  • تمنع الإصابة الشديدة الخروج في طلعة.
  • جميع خانات الحملات مشغولة.
  • الحملات المستقلة غير متاحة في اللعب الجماعي.

<en> Danger and defeat

<en> Weapon level gap

  • <en> Danger compares the weapon tier with the location, within ±2 levels.
  • <en> Each missing tier adds +0.1 damage taken. Each tier above multiplies it by 0.97.
  • <en> Armor absorbs up to 60% of the damage.

<en> Defeat

  • <en> The resident returns with 10% health and the weapon loses 15% of its durability.
  • <en> Armor covers 50% of the defeat chance.
  • <en> Defeat on Aggressive can cause a heavy injury (30%).

<en> No weapon or no ammo

  • <en> Fighting without a gun deals ×1.5 damage.
  • <en> Defeat chance ×1.5.
  • <en> The resident withdraws below 50% health.

<en> Location depletion

  • <en> Each encounter drains 4% of the location yield.
  • <en> It recovers 10% per hour.
  • <en> The yield never drops below 35%.

<en> Goals and loot

<en> Each goal has its own loot table. The chances are within that goal.
