Talents

Base -> Mastery -> Combo I..IV. A Combo skill needs ALL of its listed prerequisites at max level.

Base - Attack (23)

Ambush

Base · Attack · max level 6

Your first attack on an enemy that has not noticed you yet deals 50–150% more damage and is always a critical hit.

Armor Breaker

Base · Attack · max level 6

Hits reduce the target's defense by 10-60% for 3 seconds. The effect helps break tough enemies but does not stack.

Assault Rhythm

Base · Attack · max level 6

Assault rifle hits have a 10-25% chance to return 1 round to the magazine, supporting longer bursts.

Battle Fury

Base · Attack · max level 6

After killing an enemy, reload speed increases by 40-120% for 2-4 seconds, helping keep pressure without a pause.

Boxer

Base · Attack · max level 6

Fist and brass knuckle strikes deal 20–60% more damage, and every 3rd strike in a combo stuns the enemy for 0.4–1 s. At level 6 strikes cost half as much stamina.

Chain Reaction

Base · Attack · max level 6

Upon death, the enemy has a 5-15% chance to explode and deal 30-90% damage to nearby enemies within 2.5m.

Cowboy

Base · Attack · max level 6

Every 6th to 4th pistol shot deals 35-70% more damage, rewarding steady pistol rhythm.

Cryogenic Rounds

Base · Attack · max level 6

Hits slow the target by 10–30% for 1.5–2 s. Does not stack. At level 6 every 3rd hit on the same target freezes it for 1 s.

Cannot be combined with

Fire Rounds

Demolitionist

Base · Attack · max level 6

Grenade launchers reload 15–45% faster and their explosions have a 10–30% larger radius.

Double Charge

Base · Attack · max level 6

Shotgun shots have an 8-20% chance not to consume a round, extending combat before reloads.

Executioner

Base · Attack · max level 6

A melee hit instantly finishes off a regular enemy (not a boss or mini-boss) that has 8–20% health or less left.

Fire Rounds

Base · Attack · max level 6

Every 8th hit at low levels, and every 5th hit at high levels, sets the target on fire for 4 s and deals 20–50% extra fire damage.

Cannot be combined with

Cryogenic Rounds

Infantry Training

Base · Attack · max level 6

Assault rifle spread is reduced by 12-30%, making bursts more accurate at medium range.

Long Range

Base · Attack · max level 6

Weapon hits on targets farther than 12 m deal 10–30% more damage.

Machine Gunner

Base · Attack · max level 6

Machine guns reload 10–25% faster and have 10–30% less spread.

Psi Echo

Base · Attack · max level 6

After killing an enemy with a weapon, your next offensive psi-skill within 5 seconds deals 15-40% more damage.

Repositioning

Base · Attack · max level 6

For 2 seconds after a dodge roll, all weapon and melee attacks deal 15–40% more damage.

Samurai

Base · Attack · max level 6

Melee attack speed increases by 10-30%, strengthening an aggressive close-combat style.

Shock Charge

Base · Attack · max level 6

Grenade launcher explosions knock enemies back by 1-2.5 m.

Sniper

Base · Attack · max level 6

The first shot after a reload is 40–100% more accurate and deals 25–60% more damage. At level 6 that shot is always a critical hit.

Street Fight

Base · Attack · max level 6

Within 1.5 s after a dodge roll, your first fist or brass knuckle strike deals 50–100% more damage and knocks the enemy back 1–2 m.

Suppressive Fire

Base · Attack · max level 6

Each hit puts a suppression stack on the target. At 5 stacks the target is stunned for 0.6–1.2 s and knocked back. The same target can be stunned at most once every 4 s.

Trench Fire

Base · Attack · max level 6

After standing still for at least 0.75 s, your weapon deals 8–24% more damage. The bonus lasts 2 more seconds after you start moving.

Base - Support (23)

Acrobat

Base · Support · max level 6

Stamina cost for rolls and jumps is reduced by 8-20%, helping reposition more often.

Alarm Specialist

Base · Support · max level 6

Reduces the chance of setting off a car alarm by 15–90%.

Crystal Magnet

Base · Support · max level 6

Crystal pickup radius increases by 20-120%, making combat loot collection easier.

Doctor

Base · Support · max level 6

Medkits and other healing effects restore 10-22% more health, increasing the value of medical supplies.

Fast Hands

Base · Support · max level 6

Weapon reload speed increases by 8-20%, reducing dangerous pauses in combat.

Feign Death

Base · Support · max level 6

Lethal damage does not kill you: you stay at 4–14% health and lie still for 10 s while enemies lose interest in you. Cooldown from 15 to 6 min.

Fortitude

Base · Support · max level 6

When health falls below 35-50%, incoming damage is reduced by 10-22%.

Hardy

Base · Support · max level 6

Stamina consumption is reduced by 8-20%, and stamina recovery increases by 10-22%.

Jumper

Base · Support · max level 6

Jump height increases by 15-45%, opening more options for traversal and positioning.

Light Step

Base · Support · max level 6

Walking speed increases by 6-18%, and movement speed while aiming increases by 4-10%.

Meditation

Base · Support · max level 6

Increases psi energy recovery by 12–30% and reduces the delay before regeneration begins by 10–22%.

Pack Mule

Base · Support · max level 6

Carry capacity increases by 5-30 kg, letting the character bring back more resources from expeditions.

Psi Feedback

Base · Support · max level 6

After using a psi skill, there is a 12-30% chance to instantly return 25% of the maximum psi energy value.

Regeneration

Base · Support · max level 6

Passively restores 0.4–1 health per second. After taking damage, regeneration reactivates after 10–5 seconds.

Scavenger's Instinct

Base · Support · max level 6

Your instinct marks abandoned supplies on the minimap within 25–100% of its visible radius. Hidden caches remain concealed.

Second Wind

Base · Support · max level 6

When stamina drops below 10%, instantly restore 50% stamina. Cooldown 60–30 s. At level 6 dodge rolls cost no stamina for the next 2 s.

Shooting on the Move

Base · Support · max level 6

Move 8–20% faster while shooting and 4–10% faster while aiming; the accuracy penalty for moving is 10–22% lower.

Stable Circuit

Base · Support · max level 6

After taking damage, the next psi ability costs 10-40% less, helping respond under pressure.

Steel Body

Base · Support · max level 6

Incoming damage is reduced by 4-10%, improving survivability in longer fights.

Stockpiling

Base · Support · max level 6

Using a consumable has a 10-22% chance not to consume the item.

Thirst for Experience

Base · Support · max level 6

Crystals grant 10-30% more experience, speeding up character growth.

Track Athlete

Base · Support · max level 6

Running speed increases by 5-12%, helping reposition faster and escape threats.

Veteran

Base · Support · max level 6

Each learned base Attack ability increases weapon and melee damage by 2–5%.
