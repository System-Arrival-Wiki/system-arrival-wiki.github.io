Skills

Skills improve the more you use them, up to level 100. Equipment, implants and status effects can raise the effective skill above that.

Combat (11)

Heavy automatic weapons

Firearm

Active

Increases heavy automatic weapon damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100. Progress is slower because of the large magazine.

Pistols

Firearm

Active

Increases pistol damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100. Also reduces spread.

Rifles

Firearm

Active

Increases assault rifle damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Shotguns

Firearm

Active

Increases shotgun damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

SMGs

Firearm

Active

Increases submachine gun damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Sniper rifles

Firearm

Active

Increases sniper rifle damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Blunt weapons

Melee

Active

Increases blunt weapon damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Piercing weapons

Melee

Active

Increases piercing weapon damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Slashing weapons

Melee

Active

Increases slashing weapon damage by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Unarmed combat

Melee

Active

Increases fist and knuckle damage by +1% per skill point (up to +100% at level 100) and attack speed by +0.3% per point (up to +30% at level 100). Trained by hitting with fists and knuckles.

Explosives

Explosive

Active

Increases the damage of explosives, grenades and grenade launchers by +1% per skill point, up to +100% at level 100.

Core (4)

Scavenging

Utility

Draft

Faster searching of loot points. Trained by completing a normal search; at skill 100 the search time drops to about 10% of the base time.

Lumberjacking

Gathering

Draft

Faster felling and timber gathering. Trained by completing a lumber point; at skill 100 the harvest time drops to about 10% of the base time.

Mining

Gathering

Draft

Faster mining with a pickaxe. Trained by completing a mining point; at skill 100 the mining time drops to about 10% of the base time.

Stealth

Stealth

Active

While crouching, reduces the detection radius: from 100% at skill 0 to 40% at skill 100. Experience is gained after leaving a danger zone without being detected; any aggro disables progress and invisibility.

Meta (12)

Ammo crafting

Craft

Draft

Mass crafting: shorter crafting time and a chance of a bonus yield.

Armor crafting

Craft

Draft

Unlocks armor recipes by skill level.

Component crafting

Craft

Draft

Unlocks recipes by skill level. Limited to components, tech assemblies and turrets.

Cybernetics

Craft

Draft

A broad meta skill with soft bonuses: shorter crafting time for implants and mods and a shorter implant install time.

Firearm crafting

Craft

Draft

Unlocks firearm recipes by skill level and speeds up crafting.

Melee weapon crafting

Craft

Draft

Unlocks melee weapon recipes by skill level.

Pharmacology

Craft

Draft

Applies to all Consumable recipes: shorter crafting time and a chance of a bonus yield.

Lab Technician

Research

Active

Speeds up learning fractals: from 1x at skill 0 to 3x at skill 100. Experience is gained for every completed research.

Construction

Construction

Draft

Speeds up construction and slightly lowers its cost.

Farming

Production

Active

Food production speed in the greenhouse: from 1x at skill 0 to 3x at skill 100. Experience is gained for every completed cycle.

Water Supply

Production

Active

Water production speed from a well: from 1x at skill 0 to 3x at skill 100. Experience is gained for every completed cycle.

Repair

Service

Draft

Repair and maintenance: shorter repair time and lower resource cost.
