Expeditions

Send a resident out from the map. The AI plays the run, and the resident comes back with loot, XP and sometimes an injury.

How it works

  1. Pick a resident and a location. Open the map in the bunker, choose a resident and a location, and check the danger preview before departure.
  2. Choose a goal and a behaviour. The goal decides what can be found. The behaviour sets the risk, ammo use and XP.
  3. Take control. The player can take over control of the resident. Starting at the nearest node on the selected map with the inventory and loadout of the resident.
  4. Wait for the return. The resident returns on their own. The notice says whether loot was brought back, lost or not found.
  5. Read the report. It lists the loot, fractals by tier, injuries, ammo spent and gear wear.

Requirements

  • Autonomous sorties unlock after the tutorial.
  • Health is too low (< 30%)
  • Can't send the character: not in the bunker.
  • A severe injury blocks expedition deployment.
  • All expedition slots are occupied.
  • Autonomous sorties are not available in multiplayer.

Danger and defeat

Weapon level gap

  • Danger compares the weapon tier with the location, within ±2 levels.
  • Each missing tier adds +0.1 damage taken. Each tier above multiplies it by 0.97.
  • Armor absorbs up to 60% of the damage.

Defeat

  • The resident returns with 10% health and the weapon loses 15% of its durability.
  • Armor covers 50% of the defeat chance.
  • Defeat on Aggressive can cause a heavy injury (30%).

No weapon or no ammo

  • Fighting without a gun deals ×1.5 damage.
  • Defeat chance ×1.5.
  • The resident withdraws below 50% health.

Location depletion

  • Each encounter drains 4% of the location yield.
  • It recovers 10% per hour.
  • The yield never drops below 35%.

Fractals

  • 25% of the rolls are ordinary loot.
  • 15% chance per encounter that an enemy fills a fractal bar.
  • The bar fills by 0.5 of a manual kill.
  • Danger and chance ×1.5.
